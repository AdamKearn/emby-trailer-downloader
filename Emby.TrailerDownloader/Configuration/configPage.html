Trailer Downloader

Downloads trailers for new movies and TV series and stores them in a trailers folder next to the media. Run Scheduled Tasks → Trailer Downloader → Download missing trailers once to back-fill your existing library.

When to download

Seasons and episodes trigger a single download for their series.
Gives Emby time to fetch metadata (trailer links) before downloading.

Trailer sources

Adds extra trailer links in several languages from themoviedb.org, so a region-blocked video can be swapped for another. Free at themoviedb.org/settings/api.
Searches "<title> <year> official trailer" and takes the first result under 10 minutes with "trailer" or "teaser" in its title.

Download

Gets around region blocks, e.g. socks5://user:pass@host:1080 or http://host:8080.
Path on the server to a Netscape-format cookies.txt exported from a browser logged in to YouTube. Helps with "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot" and age-restricted trailers.

Advanced

yt-dlp needs a JavaScript runtime for full YouTube support.
